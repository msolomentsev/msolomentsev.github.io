I work in the field of power electronics, which is ubiquitous but generally poorly understood. 

Power electronics is a young field, but mature in many ways.

Most applications are commodity AKA low margin aka low leverage. Increasing performance is not that helpful, and difficult to make a business of it unless you can operate at massive scale. Anker doing this for consumer electroincs is the classic example. Non-intuitive example: utility scale solar inverters. You'd think that there would be demand for ultra-optimized solutions, getting more power out of limited PV. Wrong! PV is cheap (yay cost curve), so producers want to deploy a lot of it cheaply. It's not helpful to get a few percentage points more out of your inverters at an increased cost when you could just  deploy more PV.
