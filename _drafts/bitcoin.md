No single image has broken people's brains more than the below chart. Knowing that A) there's an asset whose value has gone up by 10,000x (really even more than that) during your lifetime; and B) that you failed to buy in on the cheap - is an incredible infohazard. You look at this and it's suddenly hard to imagine slaving away at your job until you can retire on your 40 years of low-risk returns from an index fund. 

IMAGE

At this point, the shift towards the gamblification of all of American life is well-documented. Every young man is buying some combination of deep OTM options, same game parlays, and prediction market positions. I posit that you can trace all of this back to bitcoin. 

The American dream isn't a steady job, nuclear family, two car garage, and picket fence anymore. It's the making it rich quick off of a deeply asymmetric high leverage bet.
