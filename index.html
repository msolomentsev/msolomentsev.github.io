I'm Michael Solomentsev.

I'm an electrical engineer & entrepreneur.

I got my PhD at UT Austin, where I studied power electronics. My dissertation was a hodgepodge of different topics mostly related to high-frequency magnetic components. While not the most legible to a layman, this paper ("At what frequencies should air-core magnetics be used?") is probably my best work (or at the very least, is what I'm most proud of).

After graduating, I founded Palanquin Power, a startup commercializing some of my PhD research. We are working to develop ultra-efficient data center power supplies utilizing differential power processing architectures. We are funded by a combination of US Department of Energy, National Science Foundation, and state of Colorado grants. I'm currently supported by the DoE's lab embedded entrepreneurship program.

Outside of my specific startup, I'm broadly interested in effective commercialization of high-impact research - from both a policy and entrepreneurial perspective. I'm also a pretty accomplished public speaker. I live in Colorado and like to spend my free time outdoors.

If you think I could be helpful, please reach out. I think it's a pleasure to hear about interesting problems.
