I'm Michael Solomentsev.

I'm an electrical engineer & entrepreneur.

I got my PhD at UT Austin, specializing in power electronics. My dissertation was a hodgepodge of different topics mostly related to high-frequency magnetics (inductors and transformers).

After graduating, I founded Palanquin Power, a startup commercializing some of my PhD research. We are working to develop ultra-efficient data center power supplies. We are funded by a combination of US Department of Energy, National Science Foundation, and state of Colorado grants. I'm currently supported by the DoE's lab embedded entrepreneurship program.

Outside of my specific startup, I'm broadly interested in effective commercialization of high-impact research - from both a policy and entrepreneurial perspective. I'm also a pretty accomplished public speaker. I live in Colorado and like to spend my free time outdoors.

If you think I could be helpful, please reach out. I think it's a pleasure to hear about interesting problems.
