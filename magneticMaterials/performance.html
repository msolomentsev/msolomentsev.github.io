Performance Factor Comparison

PF = f · Bpk, where Bpk is the flux density that produces the loss target set below at that frequency — higher is better. Each curve is drawn only across the material's own fitted Steinmetz range; breaks in a line mark piecewise segment boundaries, where the coefficients change and PF jumps. Bpk is not clamped to BSAT.

Note that these are all back-calculated from Steinmetz parameters, which may not have an accurate fit especially at the ends of their frequency ranges. Treat these figures as ballpark estimates, not gospel!
